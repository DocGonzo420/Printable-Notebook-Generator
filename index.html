How it works

From blank to bound in three steps

Nothing to install and nothing to sign up for. The page is drawn in your browser and sent straight to your printer.

  1. Choose a ruling

    Lined for writing, dots for planning, squares for sums, staves for songs.

  2. Set it to fit

    Pick A4, Letter or A5 and slide the spacing from 4 mm to 10 mm. The preview updates as you go.

  3. Print and bind

    Print as many copies as you need, then staple, punch or clip them into a notebook.

Printing tips

Get the spacing you asked for

Pages are drawn as vectors at true size, so a 5 mm grid measures 5 mm, provided your printer isn't told to rescale it.

Which print settings should I use?

Set scale to 100% (sometimes called “Actual size”), turn off “Fit to page”, and switch off headers and footers. Match the paper size in the dialog to the size you picked here.

Can I save a PDF instead of printing?

Yes. Choose “Save as PDF” as the destination in your browser's print dialog. The PDF keeps the lines as vectors, so it stays sharp at any zoom.

How do I print on both sides?

Set the number of copies you want and turn on two-sided printing in the dialog. Every ruling is centred on the sheet, so front and back line up.

Why is there a blank border around the ruling?

Most home printers can't print to the edge of the sheet. The ruling stops 8 mm short so nothing gets cut off.
